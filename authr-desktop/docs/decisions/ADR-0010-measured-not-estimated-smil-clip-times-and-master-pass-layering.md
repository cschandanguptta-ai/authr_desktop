# ADR-0010 — Measured, not estimated: SMIL clip times from per-fragment synthesis; chain layers 2-3 once more on the joined master

**Status:** Accepted (P4)
**Context:** The plan §7.6 requires read-along exports that pair "each text fragment with its audio segment" and a mastering chain (§7.5/Table 25) that runs "in fixed order per chapter segment and once more across the joined master". Two readings were possible: estimate clip times by word-count proportion (fast, drifts) or synthesize per fragment and measure (exact, needs one synthesis call per fragment); similarly the whole-master chain pass could re-duck the ambient bed or not.
**Decision:** (1) The renderer synthesizes **each fragment separately** and derives every SMIL `clipBegin`/`clipEnd` from the **measured** segment durations; the synthesized segment sum is the canonical chapter duration, and the delivered MP3's probed duration is only a 0.25 s cross-check (LAME encoder padding ≈ 50 ms legitimately sits between the two). (2) Layer 1 (ambience ducking) runs **per chapter segment** with the project bed; the whole-master pass re-runs **layers 2-3 only** — the ambience is already in the material, and a second sidechain mix would double it. (3) The read-along surface (SMIL/XHTML) always carries the **canon** fragment text; only the synthesizer input gets the normalized (respelled) text, so display and audio stay aligned 1:1.
**Alternatives considered:** word-proportional clip estimates (rejected — drift accumulates and highlight sync degrades on long chapters); one synthesis per chapter with interpolated timings (rejected — same drift, no measured anchors); re-ducking the bed on the master (rejected — doubles ambience energy and can push the ACX noise floor red).
**Consequences:** One synthesis call per fragment (cheap: espeak-ng measured 587.6× real time on the gate; Pocket TTS 7.3× on the P0 spike, both ≫ the 2× gate); the timing sanity check is anchor-true, not proportion-true; `change-impact.md` pins the duration source so nobody swaps it back to the MP3 probe.
**Do not change unless:** the plan's §7.5/§7.6 contract changes.
**Evidence:** `engine/audio/AudiobookRenderer.kt` (fragment loop, master pass), `SmilExporter.timingSanity`, `SmilExporterTest`, `AudiobookRendererTest`, gate log 2026-11-11 (`download/Authr-OS-3.0-P4-Audiobook-Gate/gate-report.md`).
